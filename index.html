What we mean ↗
01 / Purpose

Built to work the other way.

Too often, senior in the pitch means junior in the work. Charities can be won by experienced people, then handed to a stretched junior team and a familiar template.

We built AMS ROOT to work the other way: experienced people stay close to your campaign and take responsibility for the advice they give.

02 / Who we are

AMS and Root, together.

Root Media

Root brings more than twenty years of charity-focused media planning and buying, with a deep understanding of supporters and fundraising.

AMS Media Group

AMS brings independent buying expertise since 1975, specialist teams and technical resources.

AMS Media and Root Media have joined forces with one focus: planning and buying media for charities. Together, you get specialist insight and real buying strength, with experienced people closely involved in your campaigns.

03 / Services

Senior judgement at every stage.

From the first plan to the final report, experienced people are accountable for the recommendations you receive.

A plan built around your supporters and objectives, using research and your own data.

Measurement and reporting

04 / Why AMS ROOT

Built around how charities really work.

Specialist, with scale. We only do charity. And we have the strength to do it properly. We understand supporters, fundraising requirements and the conversations you have with trustees.

Senior by design. Experienced practitioners work directly on your strategy and planning, stay involved throughout the campaign, and take responsibility for the advice we give.

Built around your organisation. We take the time to understand your objectives, approval processes, creative relationships and the legal, regulatory and governance requirements around your work. Wherever possible, we plan media and creative together with your partners.

A promise about how we work

Your budget shapes the campaign. It doesn’t limit your access to senior expertise.

Our senior team brings over 75 years of collective experience. They work directly on strategy and planning, stay involved throughout the campaign and take responsibility for the advice we give. You should be able to question our advice, discuss the alternatives and understand why we recommend a particular course of action.
